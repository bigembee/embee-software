Langlang

Research

An exploration into how AI and language technology can address a distinctly African problem: making local languages more accessible and preserving them in digital spaces.

What I Learned: As an online student of Jeromy Howard (FastAi), I decided to take on my first real project with Langlang. Pushed me to think beyond generic datasets and generic models. This wasn't about reproducing benchmark NLP systems it was about understanding what accessibility and cultural preservation actually mean in code. I learned that the hardest part of AI isn't always the models; it's understanding the problem deeply enough to know what to build.
